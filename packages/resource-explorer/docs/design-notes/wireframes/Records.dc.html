One record, two kinds

Curate's commit already is a record: an append-only row — who, when, what was selected, what the manifest said, with steps writing their outcomes to it as they land. Strip the steps and that is a report. So a report is not a new type; it is the kind of record that has nothing to do but be true. They list together, newest first, under the resource they are about.

What both kinds carry

A name, a date, a server-stamped author, the selection as a snapshot of names, and the provenance of every number in it. Append-only; a correction is a new record that names what it corrects.

What only a catalogue record carries

Steps, their outcomes as they land, and a remedy when one fails. A report has no steps, which is why it needs no worker and can be written by anyone signed in, in one press.

Where this sits. Records belong to the resource, beside the journal and the verdict trail, not inside them — the journal is prose testimony and a thirty-two-row table inside it fights what it is for. A journal entry may cite a record; that is the link between what someone thought and what was true when they thought it.
