The four rules the rows follow
1 · the evidence is the sentence, not a score

a compose service with a Dockerfile beats 75% by itself, because it tells you what to check. The percentage stays, after the words, for the person comparing two rows. A row whose only evidence is a number cannot be judged without opening the table — which is the thing the tree exists to avoid.

2 · agreement counts as evidence, and the queue runs weakest first

75% and 45% from two unrelated methods is better evidence than a single 90%, so agreement feeds the ordering rather than sitting beside it — which sinks coco-mds/ to the bottom, because the shipped sort runs weakest first and that is the better choice. #86's comment says why: “ordering by confidence puts the weakest clusters first without hiding one”. A review queue should open on what needs attention; I had drawn this strongest-first, which is a catalogue to browse, not a queue to work. The label becomes by evidence rather than by confidence, since agreement is now in it.

3 · weakness is stated, not hidden

notebooks/ is a real proposal and a bad one, and the row says so in words: proposed on directory cohesion alone — nothing declares it and nothing deploys it. With reject as the emphasised action, because on this row it is the more likely correct answer. A tree that only encourages accepting is a tree that manufactures a catalogue.

4 · absence is a state, in both columns

none declared in the ports column, never an empty cell — and it is a claim about the deployment artifacts, not about the component. kind not established where no detector could name a type. The 3 unattributable ports are counted apart rather than attached to a guess, which is #85's own decision and the right one.
