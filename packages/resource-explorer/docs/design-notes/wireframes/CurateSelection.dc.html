Two · the blueprints, and why they behave differently
Live, today: 4 of 87 component paths reviewed · 0 of 12 clusters in the logical reading reviewed
The second clause names twelve clusters and opens nothing. Counts open what they counted.

And a blueprint verdict is not like a component verdict. A component is keyed by path, so its verdict holds whatever reading you were in when you found it. A cluster is keyed perspective::cluster_name — it only exists inside one reading. So the same table holds two identity regimes, and the screen has to say which it is showing or a curator will expect a blueprint verdict to carry across readings the way a component's does.

What this leaves open

Whether accepting a cluster means anything in Egeria yet. Component acceptance catalogues; a blueprint's equivalent act is not ruled, and I am not inventing one — the row above records a curator's judgement and says nothing about publishing it. If a cluster should become a SolutionBlueprint, that is a type decision for the project owner, and the SoftwareLibrary mistake says not to name a type before anyone has verified it.
