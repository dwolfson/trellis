The answer is in two places and the fact is in neither

Seven points, one cause. The owner says “the question focus is right; the facts are hard to find”, and the code says why, more sharply than the complaint does: the Questions row renders the answer and cannot reach the members at all, while Dashboard · by question renders the same sentence from the same envelope so that it can offer a detail disclosure whose payload opens in a 290px rail. Two panes hold the answer; the fact is two clicks away, in the pane the person did not start in, in a column too narrow for it.

What a stage page renders more than once today
the answer sentence Questions row · Dashboard by-question head · the evidence rail's headline list — one string, three surfaces
findings and counts under the question, again under the analysis, a third time in the rail
disagreements computed twice from two sources, emitting the identical sentence
descriptions first sentence on the survey row, the whole thing again behind definition history; the analysis's own description a third time on the dashboard section
re-run three call sites, three interfaces — the priced popover, an unpriced dialog link, the batched depth-offer table
perspectives on every Questions row, wrapping; absent from Survey and from Dashboard by-analysis — the inverse duplication

Point 6 is not a tidiness complaint. Each of these is a second place the same fact can be wrong in, and this project has spent two weeks finding exactly that class of defect in the data. The page has it in the layout.

The spine

One object: the analysis. One entry: the question. One place for the fact: the pane, under the answer.

Everything below follows from those three, and each of the owner's seven points lands on one of them.

Questions absorbs Dashboard · by question

They render the same answer from the same envelope, so they are one pane. The Questions row gains the detail disclosure the dashboard had, and it opens in place, under the answer, in the pane.

That is points 9 and 10 answered by the same move: summary with detail, collapsed by default, and the fact one click from the answer rather than two clicks and a pane away. It also deletes the first three duplications outright.

Survey becomes Survey & analyses

Definitions and the analyses they run, in one index — because an analysis that answers no catalogued question currently has no home in /next at all until a verdict makes the depth offer mention it.

Point 1. The old UI's per-analysis cards were right to exist; what was wrong with them was the two hundred words of prose above the buttons, which is point 3.

Dashboard keeps only what it alone does

The by-analysis view survives under its own name, By analysis, because reading a repository one analysis at a time is a real second question. The toggle goes: a view that is also a tab is a tab.

Measured, but no question asks moves here with it — it is a statement about analyses, and it belongs beside them rather than trailing the question list.

The rail keeps provenance, and only provenance

Evidence — what produced this, when, from how many measurements — is narrow by nature and belongs in 290px. Rows do not.

The code already admits why members went there: the Analysis pane “held an empty ask box and eighteen hundred pixels of nothing”. The rail was chosen because the pane was empty, not because the rail fits — and the pane is no longer empty.

And the four that are copy or a missing clause
2 · granularity A definition row says how many of its steps fetch: 7 steps · none fetch beside 4 steps · 2 fetch. That one clause is what stops a zero-fetch bundle and a genuine fetch reading as peers — and it is the axis the tiering already turns on.
6 · other stages Two different facts render identically today. Either these matched this stage's questions but belong to other stages, or the stage filter did not resolve and the route returned every definition for the technology type — in which case say the stage filter did not resolve · showing all 11 for this technology type. A fast path must not lie.
7 · perspectives Every pane either filters by them or says it does not. Survey sends them — the route already honours them and /next simply never passes them. By analysis says not filtered by perspective. And nothing held renders as no perspective held · everything shown, never as a filtered state that happens to match all.
4 · visuals Yes, but under one rule, because of why they were removed: a tile read Health 82/100 while the card beneath it said 82.2. A visual may only show a number the row beneath it also shows, from the same value, rounded the same way. Under that rule the radar can come back; without it, it will disagree with its own caption again.
