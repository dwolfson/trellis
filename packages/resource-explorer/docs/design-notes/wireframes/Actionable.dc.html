Point 3

A gap in the analytic is our job, not the reader's

Two years of honesty rules have produced a surface that is scrupulous about what it cannot say and silent about what to do. “responsiveness — not established. Not measurable from what is collected — issue and pull-request response times are not gathered” is a true sentence about the analysis put in front of someone who wanted to know about the repository. There is nothing they can do with it, because the work it implies is ours.

The rule that got us here is right and stays: absence and failure are states, never blanks. What was missing is the other half — whose absence it is.

A finding about the repository

elephant factor — sole. One contributor accounts for half of all commits. That is about the thing being investigated. It belongs on the page, and it should say where it goes next.

A finding about the analysis

responsiveness — not established. Response times are not gathered. That is about us. It belongs in a gaps list the project owns, and on the page it collapses to one line.

So the eleven-row wall of not established becomes, on the resource:

3 of 11 community measures cannot be computed from what is collected — what is missing, and why ›. Not a finding about this repository.

Same information, one line, and the clause at the end is what makes it readable: it tells the person this is not their problem. Behind the link, the same three sentences, plus the one thing the wall never had — an RFA against the analysis, so the gap is on a work list belonging to whoever maintains it rather than on a page belonging to someone who cannot fix it.

And every finding about the repository names where it goes

Three destinations, and the third is as important as the others — because a finding that implies no action is not a failure, and pretending otherwise is how every dashboard turns into a chore list nobody reads.

A judgement is not a task

One contributor wrote 93% has no correct action — whether it matters depends on what the repository is for, which is why it is an Enrichment field and not a work item. The destination link goes to the judgement it informs, and the judgement already carries the measurement it was made against, so when the number moves the judgement says review — evidence moved. That machinery shipped in #34; this is the road to it.

Two analyses disagreeing is never the reader's problem

8,756 / 8,362 — they may not be measuring the same thing is a true and careful sentence, and there is nothing a curator can do about it. It stays visible — a number nobody can reconcile must not be quietly picked between — but it is marked ours, and it appears on the gaps list with the two analyses named.

This also puts a number on the owner's instinct that the analytics need work: the gaps list is that number, accumulated by the interface rather than asserted in a meeting.
